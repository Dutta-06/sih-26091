# Required Documents

> Prototype checklist summarising documents commonly requested for concessional enterprise loans. This is
> NOT official guideline text; the State Channelising Agency (SCA) or lending bank issues the definitive list.

## Documents required for all applicants

- Identity proof (Aadhaar card or voter ID)
- Address proof
- Recent passport-size photographs
- Bank account passbook or cancelled cheque
- Caste or community certificate, where the scheme targets a category
- Income certificate
- Project report with project cost and repayment plan
- Quotations for machinery, equipment or animals to be purchased

## Additional documents for the Micro Finance Scheme

- Self-help group membership certificate, if applying through a group

## Additional documents for the Term Loan Scheme

- Udyam registration certificate
- Proof of business premises or land (ownership, lease or consent letter)
- Licences applicable to the activity
- Guarantor or security documents as asked by the lender
