# Term Loan Scheme Guidelines
- **Objective**: Concessional medium-scale income generation for projects between ₹1.40 Lakh and ₹50.00 Lakh.
- **Beneficiary Margin**: 10% self-contribution; State Channelizing Agency finances remaining 90% (max ₹45.00 Lakh).
- **Terms**: Concessional interest rate of 8.0% p.a., 7-year repayment tenure with a 6-month moratorium.
- **Security Norms**: Hypothecation of assets created out of loan; personal guarantee per SCA norms.
