# Application Process

> Prototype summary of the application flow implied by the problem statement (SIH 26091). This is NOT
> official guideline text; the State Channelising Agency (SCA) or lending bank defines the actual procedure.

## Application process steps

1. Confirm the business activity and the available margin money (your own contribution).
2. Check the scheme tier: the project cost decides whether the Micro Finance Scheme or the Term Loan Scheme applies.
3. Prepare a simple project report with the project cost, the capital expenditure and working capital split, and the repayment schedule.
4. Collect the required documents and self-attest the copies.
5. Complete the loan application form and submit it with the documents to the channelising agency or lending bank.
6. Attend verification: the agency checks the documents, may visit the business site and appraises the project report.
7. Receive the sanction letter stating the approved loan amount and terms.
8. Deposit or show proof of your margin contribution and sign the loan agreement.
9. Receive disbursement, usually paid directly to suppliers or into your bank account in stages.
10. Start repayment after the moratorium; keep receipts for every installment.

## After disbursement

Use the loan only for the approved project. Keep records of purchases and sales. If repayment becomes
difficult, contact the agency before an installment is missed.
