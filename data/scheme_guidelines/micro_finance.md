# Micro Finance Scheme Guidelines
- **Objective**: Concessional lending for ultra-micro income generation activities up to ₹1.40 Lakh project cost.
- **Beneficiary Margin**: Exactly 10% self-contribution; SCA provides 90% (max ₹1.25 Lakh).
- **Terms**: Concessional interest rate of 6.5% p.a., 3-year repayment tenure with a 3-month moratorium.
- **Pre-payment Penalty**: Nil. Concessional credit designed to prevent indebtedness.
