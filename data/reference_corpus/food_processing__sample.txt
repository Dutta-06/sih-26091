Food processing - small-batch agro-processing unit. Common rural
micro-enterprise sub-niches include grain milling and pulse processing
(flour, dal) for a local catchment too small to be served by a large
mill, spice grinding and packaging sold under a small local brand, and
seasonal fruit/vegetable preservation (pickles, jams, sun-dried produce)
timed to local harvest gluts. Grain milling tends to reach high
saturation quickly in any village with year-round cereal demand, while
seasonal preservation and small-batch branded spice packaging remain
lower-saturation options for longer, since they require more active
marketing rather than simply serving walk-in demand.
