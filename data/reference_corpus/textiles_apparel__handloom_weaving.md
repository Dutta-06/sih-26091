# Sector: textiles_apparel - Handloom Weaving Unit

A handloom unit weaves cotton or silk fabric on one or more looms, either on job-work for a master weaver or independently. Weaving clusters tend to have many looms; villages near a cluster may have yarn and dyeing access with fewer looms.

## Sub-niche: Job-work weaving for master weavers
Weaving on the handloom to order using yarn supplied by a master weaver, paid per piece. Low marketing effort and working capital, but limited bargaining power.

## Sub-niche: Occasion sarees and festival textiles
Weaving higher-value handloom sarees and occasion wear, with margins concentrated around festival and wedding months.

## Sub-niche: Home furnishing fabrics
Handloom towels, bedsheets, table linen and curtains sold through weekly markets, handloom cooperatives or handicraft outlets.

## Sub-niche: Direct sales through cooperatives and exhibitions
Selling through handloom cooperatives, government handloom exhibitions and online marketplaces to earn more than job-work rates.
