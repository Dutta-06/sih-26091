Beekeeping - small apiary unit. A starter unit typically covers 10-20 bee
boxes, with income from honey sale and, at larger scale, pollination
services to nearby orchard or mustard-growing farmers. Sub-niches include
raw honey sold locally in bulk to traders (lower margin, low marketing
effort), bottled/branded honey sold directly to consumers (higher margin,
requires basic packaging and a sales channel), and beeswax by-product
sale to candle or cosmetic makers. This category tends to remain
low-saturation in most rural clusters, since it requires specific
training and is far less commonly taken up than dairy or food processing.
