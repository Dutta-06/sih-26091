# Sector: food_processing - Mustard Oil Mini Expeller Unit

A mini expeller oil mill crushes mustard or other oilseeds into cold-pressed oil and oil cake. Oilseed availability after harvest and local preference for loose versus packed oil drive demand.

## Sub-niche: Custom oilseed crushing for farmers
Crushing mustard seed brought by farmers for their household oil on a per-kilogram charge. Steady walk-in demand in oilseed-growing villages, with little working capital.

## Sub-niche: Packed cold-pressed mustard oil for kirana stores
Buying mustard seed at harvest, crushing it in the expeller, and selling bottled or tinned kachi ghani oil to kirana stores. Needs storage capital and consistent quality.

## Sub-niche: Mustard oil cake as cattle feed
Selling mustard oil cake from the expeller to dairy farmers as a feed ingredient. A by-product that improves unit economics where dairy is common.
