# Sector: food_processing - Flour Mill (Atta Chakki)

A village flour mill (atta chakki) grinds wheat, maize and other grains. Most rural chakkis serve walk-in household custom milling, so the number of existing chakkis nearby matters a great deal.

## Sub-niche: Custom household grain milling
Grinding grain brought by households at the chakki for a milling charge. Reliable demand, but typically contested wherever a flour mill already operates within walking distance.

## Sub-niche: Packed fresh atta for local shops
Milling purchased wheat and selling packed fresh atta flour to kirana stores and nearby town customers who value freshness.

## Sub-niche: Multigrain, besan and spice grinding
Adding pulse (besan), multigrain flour or spice grinding to the flour mill to serve demand that a plain wheat chakki does not.

## Sub-niche: Cattle feed and dalia processing
Coarse grinding of grain into dalia or crushed cattle feed for dairy households using the flour mill.
