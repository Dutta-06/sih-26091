# Sector: services - Beauty Parlour

A village beauty parlour offers grooming services mainly for women. Training, hygiene and word of mouth matter; demand rises sharply in the wedding season.

## Sub-niche: Bridal makeup and wedding packages
Bridal and family makeup packages booked for weddings, including home visits. High ticket size and strongly seasonal.

## Sub-niche: Everyday grooming services
Threading, waxing, facials and haircuts for regular parlour customers, giving steady low-ticket income between wedding seasons.

## Sub-niche: Mehendi and festival services
Mehendi application and festival-season beauty services offered at home or in the parlour.

## Sub-niche: Beauty training for local women
Short training courses in basic beauty services for young women in the area.
