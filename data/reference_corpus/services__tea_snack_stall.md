# Sector: services - Tea and Snack Stall

A tea stall serves tea and simple snacks at a spot with steady footfall. Location decides almost everything, since most customers buy on impulse while passing.

## Sub-niche: Bus stand and highway tea stall
Tea and snacks for commuters and drivers at bus stops, highway junctions or jeep stands with steady traffic.

## Sub-niche: Market-day and haat stall
Serving tea and snacks to buyers and traders on weekly haat days and at mandi yards where crowds gather.

## Sub-niche: Institutional tea supply
Supplying tea and snacks to nearby offices, schools, bank branches or construction sites on a regular order basis.

## Sub-niche: Breakfast and evening snacks counter
Adding breakfast items or evening fried snacks to the tea stall to extend earning hours beyond tea sales.
