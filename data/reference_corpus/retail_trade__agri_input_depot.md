# Sector: retail_trade - Agri-Input (Seed and Fertiliser) Retail Depot

An agri-input depot sells seed, fertiliser and crop-protection products to surrounding farmers, subject to licences for seed, fertiliser and pesticide dealing.

## Sub-niche: Seed and fertiliser retail for the sowing seasons
Stocking certified seed and fertiliser ahead of kharif and rabi sowing for farmers who otherwise travel to the block headquarters.

## Sub-niche: Crop-protection products with basic advisory
Selling pesticides and bio-inputs together with simple crop-advisory support, which builds farmer trust in the agri-input depot.

## Sub-niche: Organic and bio-input supply
Supplying vermicompost, bio-fertilisers and organic inputs to farmers shifting to low-chemical practices.

## Sub-niche: Small farm tools and sprayer rental
Selling hand tools and renting sprayers to farmers during peak spraying periods.
