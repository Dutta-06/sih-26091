# Sector: textiles_apparel - Tailoring and Garment Stitching Unit

A tailoring unit with one or more sewing machines stitches garments to order. Skill, finishing quality and seasonal peaks around festivals, weddings and school sessions shape demand.

## Sub-niche: Ladies suit and blouse stitching
Custom stitching of blouses, suits and lehengas for women, often preferred from a woman tailor in the village. Peaks around festivals and weddings.

## Sub-niche: School uniform orders
Bulk stitching of school uniforms for local schools before the academic session. Brings concentrated seasonal volume to a tailoring unit.

## Sub-niche: Alterations and repairs
Quick alterations and repairs of ready-made garments. Low ticket size but frequent walk-in demand.

## Sub-niche: Job-work stitching for garment units and boutiques
Stitching cut pieces on job-work for town boutiques or garment traders at a per-piece rate.
