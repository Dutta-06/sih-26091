Handloom - single-loom weaving unit. A typical entry point is a
single-loom household unit producing regional cotton or silk textiles,
either for a master weaver on a job-work basis or independently for
direct local sale. Sub-niches include plain utility cloth (lower margin,
steadier local demand), festival/occasion textiles (higher margin,
seasonal demand concentrated around specific months), and small-batch
home-furnishing textiles sold through a nearby weekly market or a
regional handicraft outlet. Saturation is typically higher in villages
already known as a weaving cluster and lower in villages adjacent to such
a cluster that have supply-chain access (yarn, dyeing) but few existing
looms.
