# Sector: services - Mobile Phone Repair and Accessories Shop

A mobile repair shop fixes handsets and sells accessories and recharges. Technical skill, spare-parts sourcing from a nearby town and trust drive repeat business.

## Sub-niche: Screen, battery and charging-port repairs
Common hardware repairs for low and mid-priced mobile phones, with spare parts sourced from town wholesalers.

## Sub-niche: Accessories and recharge counter
Selling phone covers, chargers, earphones and memory cards along with mobile recharges and digital payment services.

## Sub-niche: Software, data transfer and app setup
Phone setup, data transfer, app installation and help with digital services for first-time smartphone users.

## Sub-niche: Doorstep pickup repair for outlying hamlets
Collecting mobile phones on a fixed weekly route from hamlets without a repair shop and returning them after repair.
