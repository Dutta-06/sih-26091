# Sector: textiles_apparel - Hand-Knotted Carpet Weaving Unit

Carpet weaving units work on vertical looms, largely on job-work for carpet exporters and master weavers in carpet belts. Payments follow order cycles from export buyers.

## Sub-niche: Job-work carpet knotting for exporters
Knotting carpets to the design and yarn supplied by an exporter or contractor, paid per unit area. The dominant model in carpet belts; order flow depends on export demand.

## Sub-niche: Durrie and flat-weave rugs for domestic buyers
Weaving durries and flat-weave rugs for domestic retailers and local markets, which need less skill and time per piece than hand-knotted carpets.

## Sub-niche: Carpet finishing services
Washing, clipping, binding and repair of carpets for weaving units in the cluster, a step often bottlenecked at peak order times.

## Sub-niche: Wool and yarn preparation
Spinning, dyeing or winding carpet yarn for neighbouring looms.
