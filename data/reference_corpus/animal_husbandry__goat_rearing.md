# Sector: animal_husbandry - Goat Rearing

Goat rearing suits households with limited land and grazing access; income comes from selling kids and adult animals, usually through livestock haats and local traders.

## Sub-niche: Festival-season live goat sales
Rearing male goats for sale ahead of festival periods when demand for live animals rises. Returns are seasonal and depend on timing sales to the local festival calendar.

## Sub-niche: Breeding stock supply
Maintaining improved-breed does and bucks and selling breeding animals to other goat rearers. Requires breed knowledge and record keeping; demand depends on how many neighbouring households are expanding flocks.

## Sub-niche: Supply to local meat shops
Regular sale of goats to nearby butchers and meat shops. Offers steadier offtake than haat sales, but buyers are few, so dependence on one or two shops is a risk to watch.

## Sub-niche: Goat milk and manure by-products
Selling goat milk to households that prefer it and composted goat manure to vegetable growers. Usually a supplementary income line rather than the main business.
