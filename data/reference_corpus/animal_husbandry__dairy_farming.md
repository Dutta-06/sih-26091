# Sector: animal_husbandry - Dairy Farming (Milch Animals)

A small dairy unit keeps a few milch animals with a shed, fodder stock and access to veterinary care. Local saturation depends mainly on whether a cooperative collection route already exists and how many nearby households already sell surplus milk.

## Sub-niche: Milk supply to a cooperative collection centre
Pouring raw milk at a village dairy cooperative or chilling centre. Low marketing effort and assured offtake, but the price is set by the collecting agency and fat content. Check how far the nearest collection point is and whether it accepts new members.

## Sub-niche: Door-step milk delivery
Direct morning delivery of loose or pouched milk to households in villages or town fringes without an organised collection route. Better realisation than cooperative supply, but depends on reliable daily delivery and a dense enough customer cluster.

## Sub-niche: Paneer, curd and ghee for local sweet shops
Small-batch conversion of surplus milk into paneer, curd or ghee sold to sweet shops, dhabas and caterers. Adds value in the flush season when fresh milk prices soften; needs basic hygiene and FSSAI registration.

## Sub-niche: Green fodder and feed supply to dairy farmers
Growing green fodder or reselling cattle feed to other dairy households in the cluster. Often less contested where dairy is common but fodder is still sourced individually.
