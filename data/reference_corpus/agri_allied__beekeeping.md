# Sector: agri_allied - Beekeeping and Honey Production

A starter apiary uses bee boxes placed near flowering crops or orchards. Income comes from honey and by-products; beekeeping training and seasonal migration of boxes matter.

## Sub-niche: Bulk raw honey sale to processors
Selling extracted raw honey in bulk to honey processors and traders. Low marketing effort but lower realisation.

## Sub-niche: Bottled local honey for direct retail
Packing honey in small bottles under a simple local label for kirana stores, fairs and direct customers. Higher margin; needs basic packaging and FSSAI registration.

## Sub-niche: Pollination services for mustard and orchard growers
Placing bee colonies on farms during flowering for a fee from growers. Depends on the local cropping pattern.

## Sub-niche: Beeswax and bee-colony sale
Selling beeswax to candle and cosmetic makers and surplus bee colonies to new beekeepers.
