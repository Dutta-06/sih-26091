# Sector: animal_husbandry - Poultry (Backyard and Layer Units)

Poultry covers backyard units of hardy dual-purpose birds and small commercial layer farms. Disease management, feed cost and a dependable buyer channel decide viability.

## Sub-niche: Backyard desi chicken and eggs
Rearing hardy improved-desi poultry birds on household scraps with supplementary feed, selling birds and brown eggs to neighbours and at the weekly haat. Low capital; local buyers often prefer these birds for taste.

## Sub-niche: Table eggs for village retailers and bakeries
A small poultry layer farm supplying trays of eggs to kirana shops, bakeries and roadside food stalls. Needs continuous feed supply and a regular collection or delivery arrangement.

## Sub-niche: Chick brooding and pullet supply
Brooding day-old chicks and selling grown pullets to backyard poultry keepers in the cluster, where many households want birds but lack brooding facilities.

## Sub-niche: Poultry manure for farmers
Selling dried poultry litter as manure to vegetable and field-crop farmers. A by-product line that improves overall unit returns.
