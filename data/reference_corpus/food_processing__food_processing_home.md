# Sector: food_processing - Pickle, Papad and Snack Making Unit

Home-based food processing units, often run by women or SHG members, make pickles, papad, namkeen and similar products for local markets. Hygiene, packaging and an outlet network matter more than capital.

## Sub-niche: Seasonal pickles from local harvest
Making mango, lemon, chilli and mixed pickles (achar) when produce is cheap at harvest and selling them through the year to kirana stores and households.

## Sub-niche: Papad and badi production
Sun-dried papad and badi sold in bulk to shops and through SHG federations. Labour-intensive and weather-dependent, but low capital.

## Sub-niche: Namkeen and snacks for village shops
Small-batch fried snack and namkeen packs for village shops, tea stalls and schools. Shelf life and packaging quality decide repeat orders.

## Sub-niche: Supply to SHG federation outlets and fairs
Selling branded pickle, papad and snack packs through SHG federation outlets, district exhibitions and fairs to reach buyers beyond the village.
