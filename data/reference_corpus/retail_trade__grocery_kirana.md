# Sector: retail_trade - Village Grocery (Kirana) Store

A kirana grocery store serves households within walking distance with daily necessities. Most villages already have several, so location, credit to regular customers and product range decide viability.

## Sub-niche: General kirana in an under-served hamlet
Opening a grocery store in a hamlet or new settlement whose residents currently walk or travel to the main village market for daily needs.

## Sub-niche: Kirana with mobile recharge and bill payment
Combining groceries with mobile recharge, bill payment and banking correspondent services to draw footfall to the kirana store.

## Sub-niche: Wholesale supply to smaller shops
Buying groceries in bulk and supplying smaller village shops and tea stalls in the cluster, acting as a local distributor.

## Sub-niche: Fresh vegetables and dairy corner
Adding a fresh vegetables, eggs and milk products corner to a grocery store where no regular vegetable seller operates.
