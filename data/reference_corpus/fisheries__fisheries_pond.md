# Sector: fisheries - Inland Fish Farming (Pond Culture)

Pond fish culture uses a village or leased pond stocked with carp species. Water retention through the dry months, fish seed availability and access to a fish mandi or vendors are the key checks.

## Sub-niche: Composite carp culture for local fish vendors
Stocking a mix of carp species in the pond and harvesting fish for sale to local fish vendors and commission agents. The mainstream model; prices depend on competing supply from larger ponds or reservoirs.

## Sub-niche: Fish seed and fingerling rearing
Rearing fry to fingerlings for sale to other pond owners at stocking time. Timing-sensitive and needs nursery ponds, but serves a need where fingerlings are otherwise brought from far away.

## Sub-niche: Live fish retail at weekly markets
Selling live or fresh fish directly at the weekly haat or a village roadside point, capturing a better margin than wholesale sale.

## Sub-niche: Integrated fish with duck or vegetable farming
Combining pond fish culture with ducks or bund vegetable cultivation to use the same water and land for additional income.
