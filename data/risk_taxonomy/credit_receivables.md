# Credit sales and delayed receivables

Keywords: credit sales, payment delay, receivables, wholesaler, retailer, kirana credit, exporter payment, cooperative payment cycle, udhaar.

Pattern: buyers such as wholesalers, retailers, exporters, processors and cooperatives often pay after days or weeks, and small customers may buy on credit. Cash tied up in unpaid bills cannot pay for inputs or the loan instalment, even when the business is profitable on paper.

Indicators: sales to traders or institutions rather than cash customers; customary credit periods in the trade; no record of amounts owed.

Mitigation: keep a simple receivables register; set credit limits per customer; prefer UPI or cash on delivery for small buyers; keep working capital for at least one full payment cycle.
