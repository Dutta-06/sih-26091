# Perishability and cold chain

Keywords: perishable, spoilage, shelf life, milk, eggs, fish, fresh food, chilling, cold storage, transport delay, power cut.

Pattern: the product loses value within hours or days. Long or unreliable transport to the point of sale, power cuts that stop chilling, and days without buyers all turn directly into spoilage losses. Distance to the nearest market or collection point matters more than for durable goods.

Indicators: product sold fresh; no chilling or storage at the unit; point of sale more than a short trip away; road access that is disrupted in the monsoon.

Mitigation: schedule sales around fixed collection times; arrange access to a chilling centre or cold storage; keep a small backup power source for cooling; plan a value-added outlet (curd, ghee, dried or processed products) for unsold stock.
