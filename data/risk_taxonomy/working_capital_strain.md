# Working capital strain

Keywords: working capital, cash flow gap, inventory, low season, instalment, moratorium end, stock build-up, liquidity.

Pattern: the unit needs cash for inputs, stock and wages before sales money arrives. Low-season months, credit sales and the end of the loan moratorium can create months where cash in is less than cash out, leading to missed instalments even for a viable business.

Indicators: several low-demand months; inputs bought in bulk; credit sales; instalments that start before sales stabilise.

Mitigation: keep a cash buffer covering at least one quarter's instalment; match the moratorium to the time needed to reach normal sales; avoid tying all working capital up in stock; track cash in and out monthly.
