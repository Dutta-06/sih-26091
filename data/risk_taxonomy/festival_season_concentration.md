# Festival and peak-season concentration

Keywords: festival season, wedding season, peak demand, seasonal sales, bridal bookings, school uniform season, idle months.

Pattern: a large part of annual sales happens in a few peak months (festivals, weddings, harvest, school reopening). Missing the peak because of late setup, stock shortages or supply delays loses much of the year's income, while fixed costs and instalments continue through quiet months.

Indicators: demand clearly higher in a few months; stock must be built before the peak; income in other months does not cover fixed costs.

Mitigation: time the launch and stock build-up ahead of the main season; save part of peak-season surplus for low months; add products or services that sell in the quiet months.
