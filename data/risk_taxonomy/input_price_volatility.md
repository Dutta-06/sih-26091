# Input price volatility

Keywords: raw material price, feed cost, fodder, oilseed, grain price, commodity price swing, mandi price, harvest season, margin squeeze.

Pattern: the main raw material is an agricultural commodity or feed whose price moves with harvests, monsoon and mandi arrivals. When input prices rise faster than selling prices the operating margin can disappear for several months.

Indicators: one commodity makes up most of the variable cost; selling price is fixed by the local market or a buyer; no storage to buy inputs when they are cheap.

Mitigation: buy and store inputs after harvest when prices are lower, where storage is safe; join group purchasing with nearby units; grow part of the input (for example fodder) where possible; review selling prices when input costs change.
