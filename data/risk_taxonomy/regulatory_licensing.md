# Regulatory and licensing requirements

Keywords: licence, permit, registration, FSSAI, NOC, panchayat, local body, trade licence, insurance, compliance delay.

Pattern: some activities need registrations, food safety licences, local body no-objection certificates or insurance before they can operate or sell to organised buyers. Delays in obtaining them postpone revenue while loan interest and fixed costs continue; operating without them risks closure or loss of buyers.

Indicators: food, animal or premises-related activity; buyers who ask for licence numbers; site in a residential area.

Mitigation: list every required licence before disbursement and apply early; include fees and waiting time in the launch plan; keep copies of approvals with the loan file.
