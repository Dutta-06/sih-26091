# Single-buyer dependency

Keywords: buyer concentration, one buyer, sole customer, exporter, master weaver, cooperative collection centre, contract dependence, bargaining power.

Pattern: most of the output is sold to one buyer or to a very small number of buyers (a single collection centre, one trader, one exporter or master weaver). If that buyer delays payment, cuts the price, changes quality requirements or stops buying, revenue can fall sharply with little notice, and the enterprise has weak bargaining power on price.

Indicators: fewer than three realistic sales channels; one buyer taking most of the volume; no written purchase terms; products made to one buyer's specification.

Mitigation: develop at least two independent sales channels before scaling; keep direct local sales (households, haat, retail) as a fallback; agree written price and payment terms; avoid producing to a single buyer's specification without an advance or order.
