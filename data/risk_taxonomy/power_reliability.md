# Power reliability

Keywords: electricity, three phase power, power cut, voltage fluctuation, motor, mill, machine downtime, generator, grid connection.

Pattern: machinery-based units (mills, expellers, pumps, sewing machines, chillers) cannot operate during outages or low voltage. Three-phase connections may not be available at the chosen site or may take time to sanction. Downtime reduces output and can damage motors.

Indicators: activity needs three-phase supply; site has only single-phase or irregular supply; no backup power budgeted.

Mitigation: confirm the connection type and supply hours at the exact site before buying machinery; budget for the connection application and wiring; plan working hours around supply schedules; consider a backup (generator, solar or inverter) where the process allows.
