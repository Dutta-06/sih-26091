# Climate and disease risk for livestock and fisheries

Keywords: animal disease, mortality, heat stress, flood, drought, pond, fish kill, poultry disease, vaccination, veterinary access, insurance.

Pattern: animals and fish are exposed to disease outbreaks, heat stress, floods, drought and water-quality problems. A single outbreak or extreme weather event can wipe out a large share of the stock at once, and recovery takes a full production cycle.

Indicators: livestock, poultry or pond-based activity; distant veterinary or fisheries extension services; flood- or drought-prone site; no insurance.

Mitigation: insure the animals or stock where a scheme is available; follow a vaccination and biosecurity schedule; keep contact with the nearest veterinary or fisheries officer; do not stock beyond the water, shed and fodder capacity.
